# `fda2023credibility`

## Bibliographic record

U.S. Food and Drug Administration. 2023. *Assessing the Credibility of Computational Modeling and Simulation in Medical Device Submissions: Guidance for Industry and Food and Drug Administration Staff*. Final Guidance. Docket FDA-2021-D-0980.

Stable document: https://www.fda.gov/media/154985/download

## Verification status

Verified against the relevant sections of the guidance during the Chapter 1 intended-use research task.

## Locators and direct support

- §IV, Definitions, printed pp. 8–9: defines `context of use (COU)`, credibility, applicability, and related terms used in the guidance.
- Printed p. 13: compares the FDA framework with ASME V&V 40 and records terminology provenance/cautions.
- §VI.D, `Adequacy Assessment`, printed p. 33: distinguishes quantifiable model accuracy from the broader judgment of whether the total credibility evidence is sufficient for the COU given model risk.

## Chapter 1 use

Direct support for treating formal COU as established but field-specific and optional in Chapter 1.
Also supports preserving distinctions among accuracy, applicability, credibility, and adequacy.

## Author synthesis / caution

FDA's `adequacy assessment` is a framework-specific construct.
Chapter 1 should not present it as a universal definition of adequacy, and the full framework belongs in Chapter 5.
