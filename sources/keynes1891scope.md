## Bibliographic record

John Neville Keynes. 1891. *The Scope and Method of Political Economy*. London: Macmillan and Co.

Stable digitized edition: https://books.google.com/books?id=5GcVAAAAYAAJ

## Verification status

Verified against the relevant digitized passage during the Chapter 1 positive/normative research task.

## Locators and direct support

- Printed p. 34: distinguishes positive science as systematic knowledge concerning what is from normative or regulative science concerning criteria of what ought to be, and separately distinguishes an art concerned with rules for attaining a given end.

## Chapter 1 use

Direct support that `positive` and `normative` are established paired terminology in the economics tradition and that the distinction is not invented by this book.
Supports the introductory contrast between questions about what is and questions involving what ought to be.

## Author synthesis / caution

Chapter 1 extends the paired terminology cautiously beyond economics to components of interdisciplinary analytical problems.
Do not cite Keynes as establishing one universal modern cross-disciplinary taxonomy, and do not equate `positive` with favorable, certain, objective, or value-free.
