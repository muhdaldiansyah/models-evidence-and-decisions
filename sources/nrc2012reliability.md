DOI: 10.17226/13395

## Verification status

Verified against the relevant read-online sections during the Chapter 1 intended-use research task.

## Locators and direct support

- Summary, printed pp. 1–4, especially p. 3: treats validation as meaningful for specified quantities of interest and in relation to the accuracy required for an intended use.
- Chapter 6, §§6.1–6.2, printed pp. 86–87: connects the nature and allocation of VVUQ activity to how model results will be used in an eventual application and decision.

## Chapter 1 use

Direct support for the proposition that the level and kind of model evidence required cannot be judged independently of the intended application and decision use.
