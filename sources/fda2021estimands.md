## Bibliographic record

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Food and Drug Administration, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, and Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research. 2021. *E9(R1) Statistical Principles for Clinical Trials: Addendum: Estimands and Sensitivity Analysis in Clinical Trials. Guidance for Industry.* May 2021. ICH Revision 1; ICH Step 4 text endorsed November 2019. Docket FDA-2017-D-6113.

- §I, Purpose and Scope, printed p. 1.
- §II, A Framework to Align Planning, Design, Conduct, Analysis and Interpretation (A.2), printed p. 4.

- Glossary, printed p. 19.

These passages distinguish the target of estimation from the method of estimation and from the numerical result; distinguish estimand, estimator, and estimate; and show that a treatment-effect estimand requires structured attributes beyond naming an endpoint.

## Chapter 1 use

Direct support for previewing that later statistical and causal work requires a precisely specified target of estimation and for keeping `estimand`, `estimator`, and `estimate` distinct.
Chapter 1 does not require the term `estimand`; its formal home remains Chapter 7, with estimator and estimate formalized in Chapter 8.

## Author synthesis / caution

The ICH definition is authoritative for the clinical-trial context inspected here.
Do not present it as the book's universal cross-disciplinary definition of `estimand`; the broader formal definition remains pending Chapter 7 research.
