This is the book's only empirical warrant for the difficulty of its own subject matter. It lets the chapter say *this is hard, and here is the measurement* rather than asserting difficulty.

## Verified locators

- p. 249, abstract: "The inventory consists of brief tasks designed to assess particular systems thinking concepts such as feedback, delays, and stocks and flows. Initial findings indicate that subjects from an elite business school with essentially no prior exposure to system dynamics concepts have a poor level of understanding of stock and flow relationships and time delays."
- p. 249, abstract: "Performance did not vary systematically with prior education, age, national origin, or other demographic variables."
- p. 264, subjects: the students are "highly international, with 35 countries represented"; "more than half the subjects had played the beer game as part of Sloan's MBA orientation program"; the demographics "are typical of the Sloan School's student body."
- p. 264: "Table 2 summarizes overall performance. In general, performance is poor."
- p. 264, Table 2, mean fraction of correct coding criteria: Bath Tub / Cash Flow task 1, **N = 182, mean 0.77**; Bath Tub / Cash Flow task 2, **N = 150, mean 0.48**; Manufacturing Case, **N = 225, mean 0.41**.
- p. 264: "They did worst on items 6 and 7, which test the basic concepts that the net rate is the slope of the stock and that the area enclosed by the net rate in any interval is the quantity added to the stock during the interval. One fifth did not correctly show the stock rising (falling) when the inflow was greater than (less than) the outflow. More than a fifth failed to show the stock rising and falling linearly during each segment, though the net rate was constant. Nearly two fifths failed to relate the net flow over each interval to the change in the stock."
- p. 265, Table 3, individual criteria on the **simplest** task: "When the inflow exceeds the outflow, the stock is rising" — **0.80**; "The slope of the stock during each segment is the net rate" — **0.66**; "The quantity added to (removed from) the stock during each segment is the area enclosed by the net rate" — **0.63**.
- p. 265: "These concepts are the most basic and intuitive features of accumulation. Further, they are the fundamental concepts of calculus, a subject all MIT students are required to have. It is possible that their poor performance arose from numerical errors in the required computations, but the arithmetic required is modest and examination of the responses suggests conceptual confusion, not arithmetical error."
- p. 278: "The results strongly suggest that highly educated subjects with extensive training in mathematics and science have poor understanding of some of the most basic concepts of system dynamics, specifically stocks and flows, time delays, and feedback. The errors are highly systematic and indicate violations of basic principles, not merely calculation errors."
- p. 278: "Many subjects appear to believe that the stock trajectory should have the same qualitative shape as the net rate."

## Cautions

- **Single institution.** MIT Sloan graduate students; p. 264 says the demographics are typical of that school's student body. Nothing about the general population follows, and the chapter says so.
- **Convenience sample with voluntary demographic reporting.** p. 278: "Reporting of demographic data was voluntary; as a result of missing data, the degrees of freedom for each factor may differ across tasks."
- **The demographic analysis is reported with threshold verdicts, and the book does not repeat them.** p. 278 describes a gender difference as "only marginally significant" and region-of-origin effects as significant or marginally significant across tasks. **Chapter 8's discipline applies to the book's own sources**: this chapter uses the study's measurements and none of its significance verdicts. The gender comparison is not used at all — a marginal result on a convenience sample, reported without an effect size in the text, and nothing in Chapter 13 needs it.
- **Published in 2000.** The chapter states the date rather than presenting the finding as current.
- **Extraction damage.** Table 3's criterion rows carry parenthesised symbolic glosses that mangle under text extraction; comparison symbols appear as substitute characters elsewhere in the paper. No quotation taken from this source contains a comparison symbol, and the parenthetical glosses are excluded from every quotation.
- Do not use this source for anything beyond the difficulty finding. Its recommendations about systems-thinking education are outside the book's scope.
