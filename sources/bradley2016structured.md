Verified against the relevant full-text sections and the EPA publication record during the Chapter 1 positive/normative research task.

## Locators and direct support

- Executive Summary, printed p. xiii: describes structured decision making as engaging stakeholders, experts, and decision-makers in a deliberative environment that deals rigorously with facts and values.
- Chapter 2, especially the formal decision-process discussion beginning printed p. 5: separates decision context, objectives and evaluation criteria, alternatives, consequence estimation, trade-offs and selection, and implementation/monitoring.
- EPA Science Inventory publication record: identifies the report as EPA/600/R-15/248 and publication year 2016.

## Chapter 1 use

Direct support for separating evidence or factual information about consequences from values and objectives used to evaluate those consequences and make a decision.
Supports the Chapter 1 warning that a recommendation should not be presented as if it followed from evidence alone.

## Author synthesis / caution

The book uses this source to support an introductory interface between evidence, consequences, values, and choice.
The EPA structured-decision process is not adopted as a universal book-wide decision framework; formal value structuring, trade-offs, and decision theory remain in Chapters 10–11.
