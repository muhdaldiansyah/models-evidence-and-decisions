## Chapter 10 role and cautions

**This report is Chapter 10's spine source**, and it carries a load the bibliography's obvious candidate cannot: `keeney1996valuefocused` could not be obtained in full, and its own source note prohibits attributing procedures to it without full-text verification.

**The value-focused framework is therefore used as reported here**, with this report's own attributions intact. The same device the book applied to Brier (Chapter 6), Matrixx (Chapter 8), and Russell (Chapter 9).

**Cited within this report and not obtained:** Keeney (1992), Keeney (2007), Keeney (1982), Gregory et al. (2012), Gregory and Keeney (1994, 2002), Carriger and Benson (2012), Carriger et al. (2013), Corner et al. (2001). None may be cited directly.

**Do not develop the six objective properties beyond *Fundamental* and *Independent*.** The report quotes the list without glossing it, and the source defining the other four was not obtained.

**Do not import the DPSIR device.** It is the report's elicitation method and is domain-specific.

**Do not treat the Guánica Bay case as a worked example in this book.** Only the framework material was read.
