## Form B — A food bank's client records

### The question being asked

A regional partnership wants to know **how food insecurity in this district has changed over the last eighteen months**, in order to plan next year's provision.

An analyst has been given one food bank's client register and asked to describe the trend.

### Supplied facts

- The register holds **2,340 client records** covering eighteen months.
- The food bank serves a **defined catchment** of postcodes. Households outside it are directed elsewhere and are not registered.
- Registration requires a **referral from a partner agency** — a GP practice, a school, or the council's housing team — and **proof of address**.
- The food bank opens **Tuesday and Thursday, 10:00 to 14:00**. It is a twenty-minute bus ride from the two largest housing estates in the catchment, and the bus runs hourly.
- Policy caps support at **12 visits per household per year**. Households that reach the cap are recorded as 12 and continue to be seen informally without a record being created.
- Records are **archived at the end of each funding year**. Only monthly totals are carried forward; individual records are not retained.
- The monthly return to the funder uses the funder's **five household categories**, which do not match the categories the food bank uses internally.
- Staff report that households in the most acute difficulty are the least likely to complete the registration paperwork on a first visit, and are often given a parcel without being registered.
- Six months ago the funder began comparing its funded sites on **clients served per month**. Shortly afterwards, this site changed its practice from registering a household once to registering each adult household member separately.

### Produce

1. **Two processes.** Describe, in two short paragraphs, the process being asked about and — separately — the process that produced these records. Then answer in one sentence: **what was this register made for?**
2. **Stages.** Name at least three points at which the record-producing process decided what would and would not appear, and say who decided and what they were optimising.
3. **Absence.** Identify one group of people who **could never appear** in this register, and explain why nothing in the data reveals their absence.
4. **Relationship and size.** State whether being recorded is related to the quantity the partnership cares about, and in which direction. Then say why another eighteen months of the same records would not answer the partnership's question.
5. **Out of scope.** One of the supplied facts describes something this task is *not* about — a case where the records changed because people learned they were being used. Identify it, and say how you can tell it is different in kind from the others.

**Stop when your response is complete. Do not open the rubric until then, and do not open Form A at all.**
