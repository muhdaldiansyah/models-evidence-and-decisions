Chapter 15 closes Part IV and removes the assumption every earlier chapter made.

## Exercise architecture

Per `../../decisions/0008`. Opening task before vocabulary; three pauses; five-defect diagnosis; cold transfer on two parallel forms; retrieval from memory; delayed retest.

**One design constraint is load-bearing.** The opening task gives the seven-year series and the fact that no capital work was done, and asks what happened — **without mentioning strategy, metrics, or incentives.** A reader who reaches for a strategic explanation unprompted already has the competence; a reader who reaches for "the network improved" has the finding.

## Transfer target

> Given a metric with a documentable date of consequence, a before-and-after series in which the metric moves and the underlying quantity does not, and a legal documented mechanism, apply the discriminator, name which Goodhart mechanisms are present, quantify the broken planning relationship as a forecast error, describe the two-party structure and identify the equilibrium, and say why the obvious remedies do not work.

### Parallel forms

- **Form A — a police force's recorded-crime clearance rate** (public safety administration).
- **Form B — a further-education college's course completion rate** (education administration).

Both supply: a metric with a dated commencement of consequence; a seven-year series; a legal, documented reclassification mechanism; a planning relationship that breaks, quantified; and a two-party structure with an equilibrium worse than the status quo ante.

Every prior transfer and contrast domain is excluded. Neither domain is sensitive in the sense used here; both concern administrative measurement rather than any individual's conduct, and no real force, college, or person is described.

Chapter 15 must not claim durable far transfer.
