# Chapter 15 — Cold-Transfer Rubric

**Do not open this before your response is complete.**

This is a review instrument, not a mark scheme. Nothing here is scored and nothing is recorded.
Read only the section for the form you worked.

Both forms have one structure: a measure that improved, a goal that did not, a lawful documented mechanism, a planning relationship that broke, and a stable arrangement that is no better for anybody than the one it replaced.

## Form A — the police force

### 1. The reading

**Cases recorded as cleared rose 44% between the pre-2019 average and 2022. Convictions at court fell 1.6% over the same period. Recorded crime was flat and officer numbers fell 4%.**

**So the measure improved and the thing behind it did not**, and nothing present could have produced a genuine improvement — no extra officers, no new unit, no new technology, no rise in the offences available to clear.

If you wrote a page, look at what the extra words were doing. Three sentences forces a commitment.

**Do not credit** an answer that reports the rise in clearances without noting that convictions did not move. That is the official reading, and it is what the funding body recorded for three years.

### 2. The discriminator, and its limits

**The date is 2019, and it is in the funding body's published framework.**

**Where you got it matters as much as what it is.** The date must come from a document, not from the series. Reading a date off the data you are about to test with it is circular, and an answer that says "the break is at 2019 so 2019 is the date" has done that.

**What you find:** three flat pre-period years — 3,120, 3,100, 3,060, within 2% of each other — and a step to 3,910 in the year the consequence attached. **A rise of 27.8% on the previous year, and 26.4% on the pre-period mean.**

**What it establishes:** something changed at the date, and the change is not in the underlying quantity.

**What it does not establish**, and this is the half of the item most answers miss:

**It does not establish that nothing strategic happened before 2019.** A gradual response to an anticipated change leaves no step.

**It does not establish that other measures are clean.** One date tested one series.

**And a series with no discontinuity is not thereby exonerated.** Absence of a step is weak evidence of nothing much, because responses can be gradual, anticipatory, or small.

**A strong answer states the asymmetry explicitly**: present is strong, absent proves little.

### 3. Four mechanisms, and which need an agent

The four, in the order the form describes them:

**Selecting on a noisy proxy — regressional.** Present. Cases enter the cleared count through a recording judgment that contains noise, and selecting hard on that count selects partly for the noise.

**Pushing the metric to an extreme — extremal.** Present. Driving clearances up moves the force into a region — every eligible disposal recorded as a clearance — where the old clearance-to-conviction relationship describes nothing.

**An action that changes the measurement rather than the thing measured — causal.** Present. Reissuing disposal-code guidance changes what is counted. Nobody's case was investigated differently.

**A party who knows what is being scored — adversarial.** Present, and the only one that required the force to decide anything.

**Now the item's real question: which would remain if the force had changed nothing at all?**

**Three of the four.** Regressional and extremal are properties of selecting hard on an imperfect measure and need no decision by anyone. The causal mechanism is present too, because the funding body's own action — attaching a consequence — is what created the selection pressure.

**Only the adversarial mechanism needs the guidance change.**

**This is the item that separates readers who learned a taxonomy from readers who understood one.** An answer identifying only the fourth has described gaming and stopped, which is the failure the whole chapter is built to prevent.

### 4. The broken relationship, quantified

**0.50 × 4,460 = 2,230 forecast convictions. Actual: 1,520.**

**The forecast is 47% above the actual figure** — or, the other way, the actual is 32% below the forecast.

**Note the direction, and check whether you worked it or assumed it.** The chapter's case ran the other way: there the forecast was too low. Here the metric is on the denominator side of what is being forecast, so the error inverts. **The direction is not a property of the phenomenon.**

**Who bears it:** the court liaison team, established for a workload half again the size of the real one — and whatever that establishment displaced, which is the invisible half of the cost.

**Also credit** the observation that this error is not visible from either column alone. The clearance figure looks like success; the conviction figure looks stable. **The ratio is nobody's report**, and that is why it went four years unnoticed.

### 5. The two parties and the stable outcome

**The parties are the police force and the funding body.**

**Actions.** The funding body: attach a consequence to clearances, or do not. The force: record disposals conservatively, or record every eligible disposal as a clearance.

**The stable combination is: consequence attached, and generous recording.**

Given the consequence, generous recording is better for the force. Given generous recording, the funding body gains nothing by dropping a published scheme and loses credibility by doing so.

**Neither can improve by changing its own action alone.**

**And now the part most answers miss.** Compare that outcome with 2018.

**Convictions: 1,545 then, 1,520 now.** Recorded crime flat. Officers down.

**Nobody is better off**, except in the accounting sense that a share of discretionary funding has moved between forces — which is a transfer, not a gain. **The arrangement everybody arrived at is no improvement on the one it replaced**, and it took an incentive scheme, a guidance rewrite, and four years to get there.

**An answer that identifies the stable combination and calls it an improvement for the force has missed it.** The force's *funding* improved. Nothing the force exists to do did.

### 6. Why the remedies fail

**"Replace the measure with a better one."** A better measure acquires consequences too, and then it is a measure with consequences attached — which is the situation you were in. **This is the commonest proposal and it sounds like a solution.**

**"Add three more measures."** Three more surfaces to respond to, and now the force chooses which to optimise. More discretion, not less.

**"Audit the disposal codes."** Every code used is in the national recording standard and the guidance was circulated to the inspectorate. **An audit finds exactly what was filed, because nothing was hidden.** This is the remedy that feels most rigorous and is the most certain to find nothing.

**Credit an answer that adds a fourth remedy and its cost** — for example, the funding body fixing the recording guidance itself, which removes the response and removes the force's ability to correct genuine local inconsistency.

### 7. The audit

**£210,000 against a £4,600,000 decision is 4.6%.**

**It cannot be screened out on cost.** Almost any chance of the information changing a four-and-a-half-million-pound decision clears a two-hundred-thousand-pound bar.

**That is not the same as being worth buying.** A ceiling rules things out; it does not rule them in.

**And you cannot compute what it is worth**, because nobody will supply a probability over what the sample would show. Assigning one and computing an expected value produces a number whose provenance will not travel with it.

**A strong answer says all three.** An answer that computes an expected value has reverted to a chapter's worth of machinery whose inputs are absent.

### 8. What the page omits

Any of: what the funding body actually rewards, and how much of the allocation turns on this measure; whether other forces did the same thing; what the force's own internal target was; whether the guidance change was initiated before or after the framework was published.

**The strongest answer is the last**, because it bears directly on item 2: a guidance change that predates the framework is a different story.

### 9. Who is in no column

**The victims of cases closed by out-of-court disposal.**

They appear in the clearance column as a success. They appear in the conviction column not at all. **No column records what happened to them or what they thought about it.**

**What that does to item 1:** the reading "the measure improved and the goal did not" uses convictions as the goal indicator, and convictions are not the goal either. They are a second proxy, better than the first and still not the thing.

**What it does to item 4:** the 0.50 ratio was a relationship between two administrative counts, neither of which is what a police force is for.

**This item is hard and is not required for a good answer.** It tests whether Chapter 4's question — what does this record leave out? — is still being asked in a chapter about something else.

## Form B — the college

### 1. The reading

**Completions rose 36%; destinations fell 1.7%; enrolments were flat and teaching staff fell 3%.** The measure improved and the thing behind it did not.

### 2. The discriminator, and its limits

**2019, from the funding body's published rules.**

Pre-period: 2,180, 2,150, 2,205 — within 3%. Step to 2,680: **a rise of 21.5% on the previous year, and 23.0% on the pre-period mean.**

**Establishes: something changed at the date. Does not establish: that nothing happened before it, that other measures are clean, or that a series without a step is clean.**

### 3. Four mechanisms

**All four present. Three remain without the census change** — regressional, extremal, and causal, the last because the funding body's own action created the pressure. **Only adversarial needs the college to have decided anything.**

### 4. The broken relationship

**0.85 × 2,960 = 2,516 forecast. Actual: 1,810. The forecast is 39% above the actual.**

**Who bears it:** the careers service, established for a caseload it does not have.

### 5. The two parties and the stable outcome

**The college and the funding body.** Stable combination: consequence attached, later census date.

**Compare 2018: destinations 1,820 then, 1,810 now.** Enrolments flat, staff down.

**No learner is better off.** Grant has moved.

### 6. Why the remedies fail

As Form A. **"Audit the census-date change"** is the one to be firm about: both changes are within the published rules and were notified. The audit confirms compliance, which was never in doubt.

### 7. The survey

**£140,000 against £3,100,000 is 4.5%.** Cannot be screened out on cost; not thereby worth buying; no expected value computable.

### 8. What the page omits

Strongest: whether the census-date change predates the funding rules.

### 9. Who is in no column

**The learners who withdrew before the census date.**

They are in neither column. They are not in the completion count, by construction — that is what the change did — and they are not in the destination figure.

**And here is what makes this form's version deeper than Form A's.** Those learners are **the group most likely to need the careers service**, and the 0.85 ratio was fitted in years when they were inside the cohort.

**So item 4's error is worse than the arithmetic shows.** The forecast is 39% too high on the numbers, and the caseload it should have been predicting includes people who have been defined out of the count entirely.

**A reader who gets this has done the best work either form allows**, and it is not required.

## The trap in both forms

**Both series have an official reading, and it is the one the organisation published.**

The measure improved. That is what went in the annual report, what the comparative table recorded, and what any analyst looking at one column would conclude.

**Nothing in the metric column is wrong.** The counts are accurate, the codes are standard, the rules were followed. **The failure is not in the number; it is in what the number is now a number about.**

## Four answers that look right and are not

**"The organisation gamed the metric and this is a compliance matter."**
Nothing was concealed and no rule was broken. A compliance response finds nothing and closes.

**"The metric was badly chosen; replace it."**
The replacement acquires consequences too. This is the commonest wrong answer and the hardest to argue with, because it is not obviously wrong.

**"Convictions are the real measure, so use those instead."**
Then convictions acquire consequences, and charging decisions become the thing that responds. Moving the incentive moves the response.

**"There's no step in the neighbouring force's figures, so nothing happened there."**
Absence of a discontinuity is weak evidence of nothing. Responses can be gradual or anticipatory.

## A note on tone, and it is longer for Form A

**Form A is set in a police force, and policing invites moral reading in a way that reserve procurement does not.**

Nothing in the form describes an individual, depicts a real force, or takes a position on any policing practice. Every disposal code it mentions is in a national standard. The mechanism is administrative: a recording unit reissued guidance about which codes count.

**An answer that treats this as a story about police dishonesty has read a different case.** The force did what the incentive made rational, in public, within the rules — which is exactly what the college did, and exactly what the water utility did, and the point of having three of them is that the shape is the same regardless of who it happens to.

**And the funding bodies in both forms were not being foolish.** Each attached a consequence to the best available measure of something it wanted more of. That is what a funding body should do. It did not work.
