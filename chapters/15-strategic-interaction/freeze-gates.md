## What is specific about this chapter

**The only chapter needing two reviewers**, one operational and one regulatory — and the only chapter where the most important review question is legal rather than technical: whether anything could be read as describing or alleging the conduct of a real utility or regulator. The manuscript's position is that nothing in the case is illegal. That must survive a hostile reading.

**This chapter inherits Chapter 1's open Gate 1**, and its `case-data.md` publication gate records the accumulation. It cannot be more validated than the case it extends.
