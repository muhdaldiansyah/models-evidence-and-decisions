# Chapter 7 Cold-Transfer Task — Form A

Status: reader-delivery copy. Governed by `spec.md` (Transfer target) and `transfer.md`.

Without consulting the Chapter 7 chapter text, the water case, or the rubric, work the situation below.

**You are the analyst.** Produce a written examination of the claim.

Every fact you need is supplied. Do not look anything up; if something you need is missing, say what it is and whether it would change your answer.

Current pilot target: **50 minutes**. This is a design parameter pending pilot evidence, not a universal standard.

## The claim

A manufacturer operates **twenty production lines**. Over a two-year capital programme, eight of them received a machine-guarding retrofit.

The safety director's report contains this sentence:

> **The guarding retrofit reduced hand injuries.**

The board is deciding whether to fund the retrofit on the remaining twelve lines.

## The record

Hand injuries per 100,000 worker-hours, before the programme and after:

| | Lines | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Retrofitted | **8** | **5.4** | **3.6** |
| Not retrofitted | **12** | **2.1** | **1.8** |

## Four supplied facts

**1. How the eight were chosen.** The lines selected for retrofit were the **eight with the highest hand-injury rates in the year before the programme was funded**. This was the stated allocation policy and was published in the safety plan.

**2. What else changed.** A **new company-wide glove standard** was introduced across all twenty lines during the same two years. It was not part of the retrofit programme.

**3. What "guarding retrofit" covers.** The programme's records list all eight lines as *retrofitted*. Three different things were installed:

| Option | What was installed |
|---|---|
| 1 | A fixed physical barrier around the point of operation |
| 2 | A light curtain that halts the press when the beam is broken |
| 3 | A two-hand control requiring both hands on buttons during the stroke |

The records do not say which line received which. Maintenance staff note that operators sometimes find ways to defeat two-hand controls when cycle time is tight.

**4. A constraint on where the retrofit can go.** The standard retrofit requires a **fixed-position machine bed**. All eight retrofitted lines run fixed-bed presses.

Four of the twelve remaining lines run **adjustable-bed machines**, which cannot take the standard retrofit at all. Those four lines have the highest injury rates of the twelve, and they are the lines the board is most concerned about.

## Produce

Write a response containing all seven items.

1. **State the target quantity** the safety director's sentence is making a claim about. Give the treatment, the comparison, the population, the variable, the time window, and the summary. Where the sentence does not say, choose and mark your choice.
2. **Compute every comparison the four numbers support.** There is more than one. State each result and say in one sentence what it appears to show.
3. **Say why the cross-sectional comparison points the way it does.** Use fact 1.
4. **Say what fact 2 does** to the before-and-after comparison, and to the comparison of the changes.
5. **Fact 3.** Say what problem it creates for the claim, and be specific about which of the three options might behave differently from the others and why.
6. **Fact 4.** State what it means for the board's actual question. Then answer this directly: **would the same programme run over ten years, on a hundred lines, fix this problem?** Say why or why not.
7. **Write the experiment that would answer the board's question.** Give its eligibility, intervention, outcome, follow-up, and comparison. Then say why it cannot be run, and — the important part — **what writing it tells you about the assumption the existing analysis is carrying.**

Finish with a **verdict** in three short paragraphs: what is not established, which assumption would change that, and what you would go and get.

**Stop when your response is complete. Do not open the rubric until then, and do not open Form B at all.**
