## Problem 1 — the delay-attribution system

A **national rail infrastructure owner** and the **train operators** that run on its network share a formal regime for attributing delay. Every delay incident above three minutes is attributed either to the infrastructure owner or to an operator, and **the infrastructure owner pays compensation of £46 for each delay minute attributed to it.**

Attribution was done by human delay-attribution clerks. **Since April 2023 an automated system has made the first attribution**, with either party able to escalate to a joint panel.

### What the system was built on

**148,000 past attribution decisions**, 2018–2022.

| Year | Incidents |
|---|---:|
| 2018 | 31,200 |
| 2019 | 32,400 |
| 2020 | **19,800** |
| 2021 | 30,100 |
| 2022 | 34,500 |

**The label the system was trained to predict is the attribution a clerk recorded.**

### What the supplier reported

**Agreement with held-out clerk decisions: 88%.**
**Agreement with a joint-panel re-review of 250 incidents: 64%.**

### Since deployment

| Quarter | Share of delay minutes attributed to the infrastructure owner |
|---|---:|
| 2023 Q3 | 41.2% |
| 2023 Q4 | 43.5% |
| 2024 Q1 | 45.1% |
| 2024 Q2 | 46.9% |
| 2024 Q3 | 48.4% |
| 2024 Q4 | **49.8%** |

**Delay minutes attributed to the infrastructure owner: 574,000 in 2022; 692,000 in 2024 — up 21%, worth £5,428,000 in additional compensation.**

**Recorded track-fault incidents**, logged by the maintenance regime and unconnected to the attribution system: **1,840 in 2022; 1,861 in 2024 — up 1.1%.**

**The system was procured, and is maintained, by the infrastructure owner.**

## Problem 2 — the station signage decision

The same infrastructure owner is deciding whether to repaint wayfinding signage at **40 stations this year or next year**.

**This year: £186,000. Next year: £198,000.**

It has six years of **wayfinding complaints** across those 40 stations. In two of the six years a partial repaint happened anyway, for unrelated reasons — the signage contractor had spare capacity at year end.

| Year | Partial repaint? | Wayfinding complaints |
|---|---|---:|
| 2018 | no | 412 |
| 2019 | no | 398 |
| 2020 | **partial** | 331 |
| 2021 | no | 441 |
| 2022 | **partial** | 352 |
| 2023 | no | 427 |

**No repaint: four years, mean 419.5, standard deviation 16.1.**
**Partial repaint: two years, mean 341.5.**
**Difference: −78 complaints, or −18.6%.**
