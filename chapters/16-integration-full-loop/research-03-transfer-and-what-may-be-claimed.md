# Research 03: Transfer, and What This Book May Claim About Its Own

Cluster 3 of four.

**This dossier is shorter than any research dossier in the book, and the reason is the finding.** Both relevant sources were promoted for Chapter 1 and verified against author-provided abstracts rather than read in full. Attempts to upgrade both failed. **Nothing new may be claimed, and this dossier records why rather than pretending otherwise.**

## 1. What was attempted

**`butler2010transfer`.** Two routes: the author's institutional page (401) and a second university mirror (connection failure). **Not obtained.**

**`schwartz2011contrasting`.** The **author's accepted manuscript** was obtained — sixty-three double-spaced pages with a running head — and **declined**.

**The reason for declining is worth stating**, because the manuscript is readable and the temptation to use it is real. The bibliography entry gives the published article's pages, 759–775. The accepted manuscript carries none of that pagination. **Citing an accepted manuscript by section against a bibliography entry that promises published page numbers is exactly the confusion the standing pagination rule was adopted to prevent** — worse than the `perdomo2020performative` case, where no published pagination is visible anywhere, because here the reader would reasonably expect the locators to match a document the book has not read.

## 2. What each source supports, unchanged from Chapter 1

Both source notes were written for `../../decisions/0008` and their bounded implications stand.

**`butler2010transfer`** supports that retrieval and delayed transfer assessment are preferable to rereading when the goal is usable reasoning beyond the original example. Its note already records that the study's materials are narrower than this book's transfer forms and that no claim of far transfer for the book's competence may be made.

**`schwartz2011contrasting`** supports that deliberately structured contrasts, which isolate a meaningful change, produce more transfer to semantically different topics sharing the same structure than surface variety does. Its note already records that contrast quality matters more than example count.

## 3. Why this matters more in Chapter 16 than anywhere else

**In every earlier chapter, transfer was the method.** The forms, the delayed retest, the contrasting cases — these were how the chapter tried to make its content usable.

**In Chapter 16, transfer is the subject.** The chapter's entire competence is recognising that an unfamiliar problem has the structure of something you have seen before.

**So the temptation to over-claim is at its highest here, and the evidence base is at its weakest.** The book has two abstract-verified sources and no full reading of either.

## 4. What the chapter may therefore say, and what it may not

**May say:** that structured contrast and retrieval are the devices this book has used, that they rest on findings the book has verified at abstract level, and that `../../decisions/0008` records the bounds.

**May say:** that `schwartz2011contrasting`'s reported result — better transfer to semantically different topics sharing a structure — is the shape of what this chapter is trying to produce.

**May not say:** that this book has demonstrated transfer of anything. **No pilot data exists.** Every chapter's transfer design carries the sentence "must not claim durable far transfer", and Chapter 16 is not the place to quietly drop it.

**May not say:** that the reader has acquired triage competence by reading. The chapter can give them a procedure and a way of checking themselves against their own Chapter 1 baseline, and that is all.

## 5. The one instrument the book does have

`../01-decisions-questions/spec.md` L492 records that the Chapter 1 exit artifact "is retained and repeated after Chapter 5 and in Chapter 16 to measure improvement in cold-start reasoning."

**That is the only longitudinal instrument in this book**, and it is a within-reader comparison rather than a claim about readers.

**It is unscored, and `../../decisions/0023` clause 5 recommends it stay unscored.** Chapter 1's own spec says the exit task "is diagnostic rather than a major book transfer gate" and that "No validated numerical cut score is assumed at this stage." Introducing a score at Chapter 16 would change what the Chapter 1 artifact was, retrospectively, and invalidate the comparison it was preserved for.

## 6. Known gaps

1. **Neither transfer source has been read in full**, after failed attempts on both.
2. **The Schwartz accepted manuscript was obtained and declined**, and could be used if the author accepts a wider pagination position — which `../../decisions/0023` clause 6 does **not** propose.
3. **No pilot data exists for any transfer form in this book**, including this chapter's.
4. **The book's claim about its own effectiveness remains, correctly, nil.**
