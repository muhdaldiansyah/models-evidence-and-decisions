## Problem 1 — the call-triage model

A **national helpline** takes calls from people in distress. Calls are answered by trained volunteers; a **senior counsellor** takes over where the risk is judged high.

**A senior-counsellor call costs the charity £31; a volunteer call costs £12.** Senior counsellors are a fixed establishment of nine full-time equivalents.

Escalation was decided by the volunteer, checked by a shift supervisor. **Since April 2023 an automated model scores each call in its first ninety seconds and recommends escalation above a threshold.**

### What the model was built on

**241,700 past calls**, 2018–2022.

| Year | Calls |
|---|---:|
| 2018 | 42,600 |
| 2019 | 44,100 |
| 2020 | **58,300** |
| 2021 | 49,200 |
| 2022 | 47,500 |

**The label the model was trained to predict is whether a shift supervisor escalated the call.**

### What the supplier reported

**Agreement with held-out supervisor decisions: 86%.**
**Agreement with an independent clinical review of 240 calls: 59%.**

### Since deployment

| Quarter | Share of calls routed to a senior counsellor |
|---|---:|
| 2023 Q2 | 8.4% |
| 2023 Q3 | 9.6% |
| 2023 Q4 | 11.2% |
| 2024 Q1 | 12.9% |
| 2024 Q2 | 14.1% |
| 2024 Q3 | **15.6%** |

**Roughly 2,140 more senior-counsellor calls in 2024 than in 2022**, at £19 of difference each — **£40,660**.

**Safeguarding referrals to statutory services**, made under a separate procedure and recorded by a different team: **318 in 2022; 326 in 2024 — up 2.5%.**

**The charity's funder introduced a reported "high-risk calls handled" measure in its 2023 grant conditions.**

## Problem 2 — the newsletter send day

The same charity sends a monthly supporter newsletter. It is deciding whether to move the send day from **Tuesday to Thursday**.

It has seven years of open rates. In two of the seven the newsletter went on a Thursday, because a public holiday moved the production schedule.

| Year | Send day | Open rate |
|---|---|---:|
| 2017 | Tue | 18.9% |
| 2018 | Tue | 19.4% |
| 2019 | **Thu** | 21.8% |
| 2020 | Tue | 22.6% |
| 2021 | Tue | 19.1% |
| 2022 | **Thu** | 21.2% |
| 2023 | Tue | 20.2% |

**Tuesday: five years, mean 20.04%, standard deviation 1.35.**
**Thursday: two years, mean 21.50%.**
**Difference: +1.46 points, or +7.3%.**

**A split test on next month's send — half the list Tuesday, half Thursday — costs nothing but a fortnight's delay to the schedule.**
