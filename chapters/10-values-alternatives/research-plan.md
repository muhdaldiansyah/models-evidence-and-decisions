# Chapter 10 Bounded Research Plan

Status: working control. Governs the four `research-0N-*.md` dossiers.

Bounded in the same sense as Chapters 2–9: four clusters, each with a stop condition, each closing before the next opens.

## Standing rules, carried forward

> **Every locator must come from reading the document directly. A fetch summary is a lead, not evidence.**
>
> **Quote only prose that survives text extraction cleanly.**
>
> **Cite the version whose pagination you can see.**

The third was adopted in Chapter 9 when the standard transportability paper turned out to be available only as an unpaginated preprint.

## The obstacle, and the resolution

**The obvious source for this chapter could not be obtained.**

`keeney1996valuefocused` is in the bibliography, and `../../sources/keeney1996valuefocused.md` records that it was verified only at metadata-and-abstract level, with an explicit prohibition against attributing "specific value-focused-thinking procedures, objective hierarchies, or formal alternative-generation methods to this source ... without full-text verification."

Four retrieval routes were tried and all failed. **The prohibition therefore stands, and this chapter honours it.**

**The resolution:** `bradley2016structured` was obtained in full — a 248-page EPA report — and reports the value-focused framework at printed pp. 5–8 and 49–54, with its own attributions to Keeney (1992, 2007), Gregory et al. (2012), and others. The chapter takes the framework **as reported there**.

This is the fourth time the book has used that device: Brier through `gneiting2007scoring` (Chapter 6), Matrixx through `greenland2016misinterpretations` (Chapter 8), Russell through `deaton2016rct` (Chapter 9), and now Keeney through `bradley2016structured`.

**It is not an instance of the demonstrate-because-unsourced disposition** that `../../decisions/README.md` has on notice, and the distinction matters: there, a practice was taught with no source at all; here, a well-sourced framework is cited through a source that reports it. `../../decisions/0017` clause 1.4 states this rather than assuming it.

## What this plan does not attempt

- **Any weighting, scoring, or trade-off method.** Chapter 11.
- **Utility functions or preference elicitation.** Chapter 11.
- **Value of information.** Chapter 11, and it is named there in `README.md`'s governed text.
- **Optimization over the alternative set.** Chapter 12.
- **Facilitation technique.** The book is not a workshop manual; stakeholder process is treated only as far as the concept requires.
- **Multi-criteria decision analysis as a family of methods.** Named at most.
- **Ethics as a subject.** `nasem2026decisionmaking` has a values-and-ethics section; the chapter uses the practical separation and does not enter moral philosophy.
- **Metric gaming.** Registered to Chapter 15 in canon already.
- **The DPSIR framework**, which `bradley2016structured` uses as its elicitation device. Domain-specific, and the chapter takes the objectives material without it.
