# Chapter 10 Values and Alternatives Case Data

Status: drafting freeze. Extension of the Chapter 1–9 case-data files, all of which remain authoritative for every value they contain.

All values here are **synthetic authoring data**. They are not industry averages, regulatory standards, design guidance, or values from any real utility.

## What this file adds

Chapter 10 introduces **no new case** and, for the first time, requires the anchor to produce a **decision** rather than an analysis.

Chapter 9 closed by listing four things the utility could not settle from evidence: fewer complaints at what cost; complaints from whom; what the money would otherwise fund; and whether an average improvement that worsens the worst days is an improvement.

This file supplies the artefact those questions land on — a committee paper — and what is missing from it.

## The committee paper

> **Hillcrest Zone — Capital Options 2027/28**
>
> **Objective:** improve service resilience at Hillcrest.
>
> **Option 1.** Replace the Hillcrest duty pump. Capital cost **£310,000**.
> **Option 2.** Defer to 2028/29 and review.
>
> **Recommendation:** approve Option 1.

**Two options. One stated objective that fails the two-part test** — "service resilience" names no quantity that could be more or less, and "improve" points a direction at a category.

## The four stated constraints

| Stated in the paper | On inspection |
|---|---|
| "The capital envelope is fixed at **£2.4m** for 2027/28." | An annual planning convention. The utility has moved capital between years **twice in five years**. **Soft — has an author.** |
| "The pump must be replaced like-for-like because of the housing." | A procurement convention. A variable-speed drive fits the same housing. **Dissolves.** |
| "Low-pressure incidents must be reported to the regulator." | Statutory. **Real.** |
| "No works between June and September." | Peak demand; the works themselves would cause outages. **Real.** |

**Two of four dissolve or soften**, and both of those have a person who could be asked.

## Objectives, once written

### Fundamental

- **Minimize** the number of households without adequate pressure during heat events.
- **Minimize** whole-life cost across the network.
- **Minimize** disruption to residents during works.

### Means, and mistaken for ends

- **Minimize regulatory reportable incidents.** *Why is this important?* Because incidents cost money and standing — a means to the first two.
- **Replace ageing assets.** *Why is this important?* Because old assets fail — a means to the first two.

**The first of those is the chapter's sharpest demonstration**, because it is the objective the organisation most believes in, appears in its corporate plan, and is not a fundamental objective.

## Alternatives derived from the objectives

| 4 | Install local storage at the top of the zone | pressure, without touching the pump |
| 5 | **Upgrade one comparable zone deliberately and instrument it** | pressure later, and evidence now |

**Alternative 5 is the same act Chapter 7 §6 proposed as an identification strategy and Chapter 9 §5 repeated.** Here it is derived from objectives with no reference to identification at all. Two framings, one act.

**Alternative 6 never appears on a capital paper because it is not capital**, and it is the one that follows most directly from the first fundamental objective.

## Where objectives conflict

Stated, not resolved — resolution is Chapter 11's.

- Alternative 1 serves pressure and **worsens** disruption: relining is a long, disruptive excavation.
- Alternative 5 serves evidence and **defers** pressure at Hillcrest for at least a year.
- Any capital option serves Hillcrest and **removes money** from the three zones scheduled for mains renewal.
- Alternative 6 serves households at low cost and does **nothing** for reportable incidents.

## Prohibited interpretations

Do not write or imply that:

- any cost figure is typical of water utilities;
- any alternative is the best one, or that the chapter recommends anything;
- the committee paper is incompetent — it is an ordinary paper of its kind, which is the point;
- the two people consulted were the wrong people to ask about facts;
- soft constraints are not real constraints — they are constraints with authors;
- writing objectives down resolves the conflicts among them;
- Chapter 7's not-identified verdict has been overturned;
- more evidence would settle which objective matters most.
