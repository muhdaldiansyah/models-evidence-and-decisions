# Chapter 10 — Cold-Transfer Rubric

Status: reader-delivery copy. Governed by `spec.md` (Rubric dimensions) and `transfer.md`.

**Do not read this before your response is complete.** It contains the answers.

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2. Both forms use the same rubric.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| The aim tested | Accepted, or called "vague" | Said it fails | Both halves named, **and** what follows for the recommendation |
| Objectives that pass | None, or one | Two or three that pass | Three that pass **and** are shown to choose differently |
| A fundamental reached | Not attempted | Asked why once | Asked until "just because", and the chain written down |
| A means identified as such | Missed | Suspected | Named, with the chain that demotes it |
| Roster | Repeated the consulted list | Added affected parties | Named an absent party **and** what their absence changes |
| Constraints sorted | Accepted | Doubted one | All four sorted, with who set each and who to email |
| Alternatives derived | Fewer than four | Four | Four or more, each traced to an objective, **including one the format could not express** |
| Conflicts stated, not resolved | Not stated | Stated | Two stated, with the direction of the trade, and **left unresolved** |

## Form A — lecture capture

### The aim

*Enhance the digital learning experience.* **No item of value** — "digital learning experience" is a category, not a quantity that could be more or less. **No usable direction** — "enhance" from what to what.

Consequence: Option 1 costs £480,000, Option 2 costs £160,000 plus staff time, and **the stated objective cannot say which better enhances anything**. The recommendation rests on criteria not on the page.

### Three that pass, and diverge

- **Maximize** the number of students who can access teaching they were unable to attend.
- **Minimize** whole-life cost of providing recorded teaching over five years.
- **Minimize** staff time spent operating and supporting the system.

The first favours whatever reaches the most affected students, which fact B says is concentrated in eleven modules. The second favours Option 2 heavily. The third favours Option 1, because migration costs 0.6 FTE for two years.

**Three legitimate readings, three different answers.**

### Fact A, taken to a fundamental

*Maximize recording coverage of timetabled sessions.* Why is this important? Because students who miss sessions can catch up. Why is that important? Because they learn the material and complete their degrees.

**It is a means**, and the demotion has teeth: fact B says 68% of playback is eleven modules, so coverage of everything is an expensive way to serve an objective that concentrated coverage would serve better.

### Roster

Seven affected, two asked — and both consulted parties are suppliers of facts about the platform.

**The strongest answer names the 740 disability-service students.** The first fundamental objective is about students who cannot attend, and that group's requirements — captioning quality, download for offline use, retention beyond a year — bear directly on which platform serves it. **Nobody asked them, and constraint 4 was agreed with a different group entirely.**

### Constraints

| | Verdict |
|---|---|
| August 2027 deadline | **Real** — contractual. Email: procurement, to confirm the end date and any extension clause. |
| All theatres the same solution | **Convention** — a 2019 estates policy. Email: estates. Fact B suggests partial deployment may serve the objective better. |
| Three-year retention | **Mixed and softer than stated** — one year is regulated, three is a service description. Email: the academic registrar. |
| No student downloads | **Convention** — a 2021 union agreement. Email: whoever holds that agreement. It bears directly on the disability-service students, who were not party to it. |

**Three of four are negotiable**, and two of those three constrain the objective that matters most.

### Alternatives the paper did not contain

- Capture only the eleven high-playback modules, at much lower cost (from objective 1 and fact B).
- Audio-only capture where video adds nothing (cost).
- Improve captioning on the existing archive rather than change platform (objective 1, disability students).
- Scheduled repeat sessions for the largest courses (objective 1, no platform at all).
- A one-faculty pilot of Option 2 with measurement before committing (all three).
- **Anything not procurement:** a note-sharing scheme, or a change to attendance policy. **The paper is a technology-renewal paper, so a non-technology answer is structurally invisible to it.**

Full marks require at least one of the last kind.

## Form B — allergen control

### The aim

*Improve allergen safety on Line 3.* **No item of value** — "allergen safety" is a category. **No usable direction.**

Consequence: £1.2m against nothing, and the stated objective cannot compare them.

### Three that pass, and diverge

- **Minimize** the chance that an allergen incident reaches a consumer.
- **Minimize** whole-life cost of allergen control across the site.
- **Minimize** disruption to production across all three lines.

The first, read across the **site**, points at goods-in rather than Line 3 — see fact B. The second points at Option 2. The third points away from a capital installation.

### Fact A, taken to a fundamental

*Achieve zero changeover cleaning validation failures.* Why? Because failures indicate cleaning risk. Why does that matter? Because contaminated product could reach a consumer.

**A means**, and a revealing one: it is measured on Line 3's changeovers, and fact B says the last three years' incidents came from goods-in. **The site is measuring the thing it can measure rather than the thing it wants.**

### Roster

Seven affected, two asked.

**The strongest answer names consumers with allergies** — the party the first objective exists for, and the only one who bears the consequence the whole paper is about.

An equally strong answer names **Lines 1 and 2**, who share the cleaning crew and changeover window: a dedicated line for Line 3 changes their operating conditions and nobody asked them.

### Constraints

| | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Maintain Line 3 throughput | **Convention with an author** — a 2024 volume commitment. Email: the commercial lead. It may be renegotiable, and it eliminates several alternatives. |
| Follow the current cleaning SOP | **Convention** — written in 2018 by someone who has left. Email: quality. This one has no living author, which is precisely why it feels immoveable. |
| Comply with allergen labelling | **Statutory. Real.** |
| No capital works in Q4 | **Real** — operational, volumes double. |

**Two of four are conventions**, and the second is the textbook case of a constraint that hardened because its reason left with its author.

### Alternatives the paper did not contain

- Dedicate an **existing** line rather than build a new one.
- Campaign scheduling: run all allergen-containing products in one block at end of week, cutting changeovers.
- Improve the changeover validation method rather than the line.
- **Act at goods-in**, where fact B says the incidents actually originated.
- Precautionary labelling plus consumer communication (a labelling answer to a safety objective).
- A trial on one product family before committing £1.2m.

**The goods-in alternative is the one the paper's format could not express**, because the paper is about Line 3 and goods-in is somebody else's area.

## Three answers that look right and are not

**"The objective is vague, so it should be more specific."** True and insufficient. Specificity is not the test; an item of value and a direction of preference are. *Reduce incidents to fewer than four per year by 2029* is highly specific and is a target, not an objective.

**"They should consult more widely."** Correct and unactionable as stated. The scored answer names **who**, and **what their absence changes about the decision**.

**"Option 2 is obviously better value."** The chapter does not choose, and neither should the response. Producing a recommendation is the failure the closing instruction is designed to catch.

## A note on tone

Two dimensions — deriving an alternative the paper's format could not express, and leaving the conflicts unresolved — are the ones this chapter exists to install, and both are commonly scored 0 by readers who handled everything else well.
