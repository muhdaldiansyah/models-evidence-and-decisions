# Chapter 10 Cold-Transfer Task — Form B

Status: reader-delivery copy. Governed by `spec.md` (Transfer target) and `transfer.md`.

Without consulting the Chapter 10 chapter text, the water case, or the rubric, work the situation below.

**You are advising the committee.** Produce a written response.

Every fact you need is supplied. Do not look anything up; if something you need is missing, say what it is and whether it would change your answer.

Current pilot target: **45 minutes**. This is a design parameter pending pilot evidence, not a universal standard.

## The paper

> **Allergen Control — Line 3 Options 2027**
>
> **Objective:** improve allergen safety on Line 3.
>
> **Option 1.** Install a dedicated allergen-free production line. **£1.2m**.
> **Option 2.** Retain the current changeover cleaning regime and review in 2028.
>
> **Recommendation:** approve Option 1.

## Who is affected

- **Consumers with allergies** who buy the products from Line 3.
- The **two retail customers** who take 80% of Line 3's output and set their own supplier standards.
- **Line operators**, who run the changeovers.
- The **cleaning crew**, who perform and record the changeover clean.
- The **quality team**, who validate it.
- **Lines 1 and 2**, which share the same changeover window and cleaning crew.
- The **regulator**.

## Who was consulted

The **operations director** and the **finance controller**.

## What everyone agrees cannot be done

1. "Any solution must maintain current Line 3 throughput." A volume commitment made to the larger retail customer in 2024.
2. "Cleaning validation must follow the current standard operating procedure." Written in 2018 by a quality manager who has since left.
3. "Allergen labelling must comply with regulation." Statutory.
4. "No capital works during the **Q4** peak." Volumes double from October to December.

## Two further facts

**A.** The site's annual objectives include: "achieve zero changeover cleaning validation failures."

**B.** Of the last three years' allergen incidents on site, **none** originated on Line 3. Two originated at goods-in, where an ingredient was mislabelled by a supplier.

## Produce

Write a response containing all seven items.

1. **Test the paper's stated objective.** Does it have an item of value? A direction of preference? Say what follows for the recommendation.
2. **Write three objectives that pass the test**, all of them legitimate readings of the paper's aim. Say whether they would choose the same option.
3. **Take fact A to a fundamental objective.** Ask *why is this important* until the answer stops being another objective. Say what the site objective turns out to be.
4. **Who is affected and who was asked?** Then say whose absence most changes the decision, and why.
5. **Sort the four constraints.** For each: who set it, what would change it, and whether it is physical, statutory, or a convention. Name who you would email.
6. **Derive at least four alternatives** the paper does not contain. For each, say which objective produced it. At least one must be something the paper's format could not have expressed.
7. **State two conflicts** between your objectives — and do not resolve them.

Finish with **the paper you would put in front of the committee instead**: no more than six sentences, and no recommendation.

**Stop when your response is complete. Do not open the rubric until then, and do not open Form A at all.**
