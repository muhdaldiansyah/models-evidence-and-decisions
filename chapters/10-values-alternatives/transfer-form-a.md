# Chapter 10 Cold-Transfer Task — Form A

Status: reader-delivery copy. Governed by `spec.md` (Transfer target) and `transfer.md`.

Without consulting the Chapter 10 chapter text, the water case, or the rubric, work the situation below.

**You are advising the committee.** Produce a written response.

Every fact you need is supplied. Do not look anything up; if something you need is missing, say what it is and whether it would change your answer.

Current pilot target: **45 minutes**. This is a design parameter pending pilot evidence, not a universal standard.

## The paper

> **Lecture Capture — Options for 2027 Renewal**
>
> **Objective:** enhance the digital learning experience.
>
> **Option 1.** Renew the current recording platform for five years. **£480,000**.
> **Option 2.** Migrate to the university's existing video platform. **£160,000** plus an estimated 0.6 FTE of staff time for two years.
>
> **Recommendation:** approve Option 1.

## Who is affected

- All **21,000 students**.
- The **740 students** registered with the disability service, of whom a substantial number rely on recordings.
- Students with caring responsibilities or term-time employment, who miss timetabled sessions.
- **Teaching staff**, whose sessions are recorded.
- The **learning technologists** who support the system day to day.
- The **timetabling team**, whose room allocations depend on which rooms have hardware.
- The **estates team**, who install and maintain the hardware.

## Who was consulted

The **IT director** and the **head of digital education**.

## What everyone agrees cannot be done

1. "The contract must be renewed or the migration completed by **August 2027**." The current contract ends then.
2. "All lecture theatres must have the same solution." Estates standardisation policy, adopted in 2019.
3. "Recordings must be retained for **three years**." One year is required by the university's academic regulations; three years is the figure in the current service description.
4. "Students must not be able to download recordings." Agreed with the staff union in 2021 as a condition of recording being routine.

## Two further facts

**A.** The university's strategic plan lists, among its objectives: "maximize recording coverage of timetabled sessions."

**B.** Analysis of last year's usage shows that **68%** of all playback was of sessions from **eleven modules**, all large first-year lecture courses.

## Produce

Write a response containing all seven items.

1. **Test the paper's stated objective.** Does it have an item of value? A direction of preference? Say what follows for the recommendation.
2. **Write three objectives that pass the test**, all of them legitimate readings of the paper's aim. Say whether they would choose the same option.
3. **Take fact A to a fundamental objective.** Ask *why is this important* until the answer stops being another objective. Say what the strategic plan's entry turns out to be.
4. **Who is affected and who was asked?** Then say whose absence most changes the decision, and why.
5. **Sort the four constraints.** For each: who set it, what would change it, and whether it is physical, statutory, or a convention. Name who you would email.
6. **Derive at least four alternatives** the paper does not contain. For each, say which objective produced it. At least one must be something the paper's format could not have expressed.
7. **State two conflicts** between your objectives — and do not resolve them.

Finish with **the paper you would put in front of the committee instead**: no more than six sentences, and no recommendation.

**Stop when your response is complete. Do not open the rubric until then, and do not open Form B at all.**
