## The source position

**One new source, obtained and read in full**: `colyvan2016voi`, ten pages, carrying the published pagination 302–309 on its pages.

## What this plan does not attempt

- **Utility functions, certainty equivalents, risk premiums.** No source; depth curriculum.
- **Any formula.** VOI is computed on the anchor as arithmetic and never written as an expression.
- **Multi-attribute utility theory.** Chapter 10 produced three objectives; this chapter uses a single currency and says so is a limitation.
- **Bayesian decision theory as a framework.** Chapter 6 declined the underlying argument.
- **Real options, dynamic programming, sequential decision problems.** Chapters 12 and 14.
- **Game theory.** `colyvan2016voi` moves into it at p. 306; Chapter 15's.
- **Prospect theory and behavioural decision research.** Chapter 6 declined to characterise the debiasing literature and this chapter does not open a new one.
- **Discounting.** Named at most.
