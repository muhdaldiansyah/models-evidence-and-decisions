## N. End-of-session debrief

Capture brief answers, preferably in the reader's own wording.

1. Where were you unsure what problem you were supposed to solve?
2. Which distinction changed your approach most?
3. Which section felt repetitive or disproportionately long?
4. Which instruction or link was ambiguous?
5. Where did you need domain knowledge the chapter did not supply?
6. Did any feedback, answer, form, or rubric become visible before you were ready?
7. What would you remove before removing the independent production task?
