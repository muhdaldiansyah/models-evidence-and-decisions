## The situation

A **regional health system** commissions diagnostic services — imaging, endoscopy, and pathology — for a population of about 900,000.
Patients wait past the target time for a diagnostic result about 31,000 times a year.

The system's capital plan allocates **£4,200,000** to diagnostic capacity over the next three years.
That figure was set in a corporate allocation across the whole capital programme, made two years ago, before any of the schemes below were specified.

Seven schemes have been worked up.
For each, the planning team has estimated the **additional diagnostic episodes completed within the target time each year**, under the system's central demand forecast.

## The schemes

| | Scheme | Cost (£k) | Additional episodes in target per year |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **H** | Reporting-turnaround workflow redesign | 90 | 210 |
| **J** | Community phlebotomy hubs | 610 | 300 |
| **K** | Endoscopy list expansion (evenings and weekends) | 1,040 | 340 |
| **L** | Pathology automation line | 1,190 | 330 |
| **M** | Teleradiology reporting contract | 1,380 | 310 |
| **N1** | Imaging centre — **single-scanner phase only** | 1,520 | 640 |
| **N2** | Imaging centre — **full two-scanner build** | 3,250 | 1,320 |

**N1 and N2 are mutually exclusive.** N2 is the whole centre; N1 is its first phase.
Any scheme is either built or not — there are no part-schemes and no shared costs.

**If the single-scanner phase is built now and the second scanner is added later, that second phase costs £1,900,000** — against £1,730,000 if the full centre is built in one go. A second enabling-works package and a second procurement account for the difference.

## Three futures

The demand forecast is the system's own, and the planning director has said in writing that he would not defend it beyond two years. Two other futures are considered credible by the clinical directors. Nobody will put a probability on any of them.

The same seven schemes, under each future:

| Scheme | As forecast | Acuity shift | Community shift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| H | 210 | 170 | 280 |
| J | 300 | 250 | 440 |
| K | 340 | 290 | 520 |
| L | 330 | 280 | 500 |
| M | 310 | 270 | 460 |
| N1 | 640 | 1,450 | 100 |
| N2 | 1,320 | 2,290 | 150 |

**As forecast** — referral volumes and case mix continue on the trend of the last five years.

**Acuity shift** — an ageing population and two new cancer pathways push referrals toward complex cross-sectional imaging, as has happened in two comparable systems. Scanner capacity becomes the binding constraint on almost every pathway.

**Community shift** — point-of-care testing and direct-access pathways move a large share of diagnostics out of the acute setting. Central scanner capacity stops being the constraint; sample logistics, reporting, and endoscopy capacity become it.

## Produce

1. **Rank the seven schemes** by episodes per pound, using the *as forecast* column.

2. **Fund down that ranking** until no further scheme fits within £4,200,000. Report the portfolio, what it spends, what it delivers, and what is left unspent.

3. **Find the best portfolio the budget can buy** under the *as forecast* column. If it differs from your answer to item 2, say by how much and explain **why the ranking produced a different portfolio**.

4. **Price the budget constraint**, using the *as forecast* column. What would an extra £100,000 buy? An extra £200,000? What would a £200,000 cut cost, and a £300,000 cut? Say what the pattern of those four answers tells you.

5. **Build a regret table** over the three futures for these four portfolios: H+J+N2; H+K+L+M; H+K+L+N1; and your answer to item 2. Report each portfolio's regret in each future and its largest regret.

6. **Identify the portfolio with the smallest largest regret.** Say how many of the three futures it is the best portfolio in. State what choosing it costs the system if the central forecast turns out to be right.

7. **Turn one portfolio into an adaptive plan.** Name what is committed now, what is deferred, and **one signpost** — the observable quantity, the threshold, who measures it, and how often — that would trigger the deferred commitment. Say what the £170,000 staging premium buys.

8. **Name two value judgments** buried in the episodes column, neither of which is a clinical or engineering estimate.

**Then write a recommendation of no more than four sentences**, naming what it is conditional on.
