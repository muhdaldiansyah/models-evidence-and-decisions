# Decision 0008: Chapter 1 Pedagogical Scaffold

## Status

Accepted (2026-08-16)

## Decision

Chapter 1 uses the following authoring sequence:

`initial attempt → complete worked example → prompted self-explanation → reduced support / fading → error diagnosis and repair → independent cold transfer → retrieval from memory → delayed retest`

This sequence is a book-specific pedagogical synthesis. It is not presented as a universal instructional algorithm, a new formal learning theory, or a reader-facing named framework.

No single source is claimed to validate the entire combined sequence.

## Complete worked example

Before full independent production, Chapter 1 gives the learner one complete worked performance using the municipal water-supply anchor.

The worked example must expose reasoning transitions rather than merely present a polished answer. It should show what is noticed, what remains uncertain, what alternative framing is plausible, why evidence changes the analysis, why revision occurs, and how later machinery is routed.

The worked-example rationale is informed by `sweller1985worked`, with the explicit caution that the direct experimental evidence comes from more structured learning tasks and does not itself establish far transfer for this book's interdisciplinary reasoning competence.

## Initial attempt

Retain a short unsupported opening attempt before the worked example.

Its primary purposes are:

- diagnostic baseline;
- exposure of default framing habits;
- commitment before seeing the expert performance;
- later before/after comparison.

Do not describe the book's opening task as a validated implementation of a universal pretesting or productive-failure effect.

The current five-minute duration is a pilot parameter.

## Self-explanation

Chapter 1 requires prompted self-explanation of reasoning and principles, not mere paraphrase.

Prompts should ask why a distinction matters, why an inference is or is not licensed, why a framing move is required, or what changed during revision.

The current design of three high-value pauses is retained provisionally. The number and placement remain pilot parameters.

`chi1994selfexplanation` is the load-bearing source for prompted self-explanation.

## Fading

Support should be removed progressively between the complete worked case and independent cold transfer.

Fade reasoning support, such as explicit prompts, labels, reminders, or routing hints.

Do not fade facts that are necessary to reason about the domain, including authority, operational constraints, measurements, eligibility rules, or other case-specific facts.

Fading should be paired with principle-focused self-explanation rather than treated as sufficient by itself.

`atkinson2003fading` is the load-bearing source.

The exact direction, number of faded steps, and location of the faded case remain book-specific design choices to pilot.

## Structural contrast

Chapter 1 uses contrasts to expose reasoning structure rather than to accumulate surface variety.

High-value contrasts include:

- same system, different intended use;
- same outcome language, different claim form;
- physical state versus recorded trace;
- dynamic but non-strategic versus responsive/possibly strategic;
- consequence evidence versus evaluative choice.

`schwartz2011contrasting` supports the use of deliberately structured contrast, with the caution that Chapter 1 does not adopt the full experimental invention-before-telling intervention.

Additional examples should be removed when they do not expose a distinct reasoning relation.

## Error diagnosis and repair

The planted-failure task is retained because it is directly aligned with Chapter 1's competence.

A learner should not merely name an error. The response should include:

1. diagnosis;
2. consequence for the claim, decision, or adequacy;
3. repair.

When useful, the learner should also prioritize which failure to repair first.

The failure set should include the Research 05 category error of treating a recommendation as identical to the decision.

This task is retained primarily because of construct alignment; Research 07 does not promote erroneous-example literature as load-bearing evidence.

## Independent cold transfer

The first independent cold-transfer analysis must be produced without the Chapter 1 checklist, worked solution, or scoring rubric visible.

A learner who can produce a strong answer only while those scaffolds are visible has not yet demonstrated the transfer target.

The refrigerated-warehouse and emergency-housing forms remain the parallel transfer forms. They must request the same core outputs, use the same scoring dimensions, and supply enough domain facts that performance does not depend on hidden specialist knowledge.

Instructional transfer and transfer assessment remain distinct: scaffolding supports learning to transfer, while the cold-transfer task diagnoses whether independent transfer has occurred.

Chapter 1 must not promise durable far transfer after four hours. It aims to establish an initial independent-transfer capability and to generate diagnostic evidence about remaining failure.

## Retrieval and delayed retest

Retrieval from memory is required near the end of Chapter 1.

The learner should reconstruct relationships and backward links, not merely recite stage names, then compare with the canonical map and repair omissions.

`roediger2006testing` supports delayed retrieval rather than rereading alone.
`butler2010transfer` supports the use of retrieval for inferential and transfer-oriented performance in addition to retention.

A delayed second attempt on the other parallel form is retained.

The current 7–14 day interval is an operational pilot window, not a claimed universal optimal spacing interval.
During pilots, form order should remain counterbalanced.

## Design parameters pending pilot validation

The following are retained but explicitly downgraded from implied evidence-backed constants to pilotable authoring parameters:

- five-minute opening attempt;
- three self-explanation pauses;
- at least 50% of active-learning time devoted to prediction, production, explanation, diagnosis, or retrieval;
- 30–40 minute cold-transfer target;
- 7–14 day delayed-retest window.

The two backward revisions in the worked case remain an instructional design requirement rather than a universal reasoning rule, consistent with the existing specification.

Timed and reader pilots may change these values without reopening the underlying pedagogical principles.

## Rubric and feedback

The eight-dimension Chapter 1 rubric remains diagnostic.
No validated aggregate cut score is assumed.

Dimension-level feedback takes precedence over a total score.
Aggregate scoring may be recorded during pilots to test interpretability, form comparability, and rater agreement.

The opening attempt is preserved as an unscored baseline artifact for qualitative before/after comparison.

## Source boundary

Research 07 promotes:

- `chi1994selfexplanation`;
- `atkinson2003fading`;
- `roediger2006testing`;
- `butler2010transfer`;
- `sweller1985worked`;
- `schwartz2011contrasting`.

Contextual sources reviewed during Research 07 are not promoted unless a later manuscript claim requires them.

## No architecture change

This decision does not change:

- the Chapter 1 title;
- central question;
- six-section architecture;
- 24-page target;
- 4-hour target pending timed pilot;
- municipal water-supply anchor;
- pendulum or student-assessment contrasts;
- refrigerated-warehouse or emergency-housing transfer forms;
- eight rubric dimensions;
- the no-validated-cut-score policy;
- the macro book architecture.

Before Chapter 1 is declared stable for drafting, `spec.md` should be synchronized so that the numerical instructional parameters are visibly treated as pilotable design choices rather than established universal optima, and so that self-explanation, fading, retrieval, and cold-transfer requirements match this decision.
