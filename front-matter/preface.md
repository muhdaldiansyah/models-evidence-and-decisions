# Preface

This book teaches one thing: how to reason about an unfamiliar consequential problem without a specialist standing next to you.

Not how to run a technique.
There are better books for every technique in here, and this one names them rather than competing with them.
What it teaches is the part that comes before technique and the part that comes after — working out what is actually being asked, what the available records can and cannot support, what a decision needs that no amount of evidence supplies, and what to revise when acting on the world changes the world you were acting on.

## Who this is for

Someone technically literate who is comfortable with algebra and willing to run a small calculation, and who has no prior training in statistics, economics, operations research, causal inference, control theory, or decision analysis.

If you have training in one of those, you will find your own field treated at a level well below what you know.
That is deliberate.
**The book's subject is the interfaces between those fields**, and the interfaces are where consequential problems actually fail.

## How the book is built

Five parts, seventeen chapters, about a hundred hours of work if you do the exercises.

**Sixteen of the seventeen chapters work one case**: a small municipal water utility during a seven-day heatwave.
The same utility, the same people, the same records, seventeen times.

That is not economy.
A new case every chapter would let you learn the chapter's technique and forget it, because nothing would ever come back to contradict you.
Here things come back.
A number you have used since Chapter 2 turns out in Chapter 4 never to have been measured.
An answer you were satisfied with in Chapter 6 is shown in Chapter 8 to have been resting on a spread nobody justified.
A plan you write in Chapter 12 is operated for four years in Chapter 17, and one of its two thresholds turns out to have been a timer rather than a trigger.

**You will be wrong in this book, repeatedly, about things you were told correctly.**
That is the design.

## What the book refuses to do

It does not derive estimators, teach test procedures, or supply a formula for the value of information.
It does not teach control-chart machinery, transport formulas, or game-theoretic solution concepts beyond one worked table.
Where a chapter's governed scope permits machinery and the chapter declines it, the chapter says so and says why.

It does not resolve live disagreements between disciplines.
Where two traditions disagree about method and agree about a conclusion, the book reports both and takes the conclusion.

**It does not invent vocabulary.**
Where established terminology exists, the book uses it.
Where the book's own synthesis is doing the work, it is labelled as the book's own rather than presented as settled theory.

## What has not been established

The book is drafted.
Nothing in it has been validated, and you should know that on this page rather than on the last one.

**No exercise in this book has pilot data.**
The time budgets, the difficulty of the transfer tasks, the number of self-explanation pauses, the choice to plant exactly five defects — all of it is designed rather than tested.
When a chapter says an exercise takes fifty minutes, that is an estimate by the person who wrote it.

**No chapter has been reviewed by a subject-matter expert.**
The water utility is fictional and its operating story has never been read by anyone who runs one.
Every figure in it is synthetic and instructional, and none of it should be taken as an industry value, a design criterion, or a regulatory threshold.

**The book makes no claim to produce transfer.**
It is built on an instructional architecture — retrieval, spacing, self-explanation, contrasting cases, error diagnosis, cold transfer to unfamiliar domains — chosen because the evidence for those devices is good.
That is a reason to expect it to work.
It is not evidence that it did.

None of this is an apology.
It is the same discipline the book asks of you applied to the book: **a claim carries what it is conditional on**, and these are the conditions.

## Before you start

Read [How to Use This Book](how-to-use-this-book.md) first.
It is short, and it contains one instruction that matters more than the rest: **there are files in every chapter you must not open until you have finished writing**.
A reader who opens them early has not cheated on a test — there is no test, and nothing here is scored.
They have destroyed the only instrument the chapter had for showing them what they could not yet do.
