# How to Use This Book

Every chapter has the same shape.
Learning it once is worth the five minutes, because the shape is doing work that the prose cannot do on its own.

## The shape of a chapter

**1. An unaided attempt, before anything is taught.**
Each chapter opens by handing you the problem cold and asking you to do something about it.
You will not be equipped.
That is the point — the attempt is a record of what you thought before the chapter, and you will be asked to compare against it at the end.

**Keep it. Do not score it. Do not tidy it up later.**

**2. The chapter, with three pauses.**
Every chapter contains exactly three self-explanation pauses.
Each asks a question and then, further down, answers it.

**Write your answer down before you read on.**
A pause you read through is a paragraph.
A pause you answer is the only place in the chapter where you find out whether you followed the argument or merely agreed with it.

**3. Five planted defects.**
Late in each chapter you are handed a piece of work containing five specific faults and asked to diagnose them.
Feedback lives in a separate file.
**Do not open it until your five are written down** — including the ones you are unsure about, which are the informative ones.

**4. A cold-transfer task.**
Two parallel forms, A and B, in domains that have nothing to do with water.
You work **one** of them, from that page alone, without the chapter, without the case, and without the rubric.

**5. A rubric, afterwards.**
Opened only after your response is finished.
Chapters 1 to 11 score dimensions from 0 to 2; from Chapter 12 the rubric stops scoring and becomes a review instrument that records nothing.
**Neither has a validated cut score, and no total means anything.**

**6. Retrieval from memory, and a delayed retest.**
You reconstruct the chapter's structure with the chapter closed.
Then, seven to fourteen days later, you work the *other* transfer form.

## The one rule

**Do not open `transfer-rubric.md`, `diagnosis-feedback.md`, or the transfer form you were not assigned, until you have finished producing.**

Nothing here is graded and nobody is watching.
The reason for the rule is not integrity, it is instrumentation.

The cold-transfer task is the only device in the chapter that can tell you what you can do **without** the chapter in front of you.
Once you have seen the rubric, that measurement is gone and cannot be recovered — not by trying harder, not by waiting, and not on the parallel form, because you now know what the rubric asks for.

A reader who opens the rubric early has not failed.
They have thrown away the one thing the chapter had to offer that reading cannot supply.

## What it costs

About **a hundred hours** for the whole book, of which roughly a quarter is production rather than reading.

Per chapter: four to eight hours, and the chapters are not equal.
Chapter 8 is the longest at forty pages, Chapter 17 the shortest at eighteen.
The cold-transfer task alone runs thirty to sixty minutes.

**These numbers are designed, not measured.**
No reader has been timed, so treat them as the author's estimate and not as a finding.

## Read in order

The chapters are cumulative in a stronger sense than most books.

Chapter 4 overturns a number Chapter 2 gave you.
Chapter 8 reopens Chapter 6's answer.
Chapter 16 asks you to retrieve the unaided attempt you were told to keep in Chapter 1, fifteen chapters earlier.
Chapter 17 operates the plan you wrote in Chapter 12.

**A chapter read out of order still works as an essay and stops working as an instrument.**

If you are short of time, read fewer chapters in order rather than more chapters out of it.

## If you are teaching from this

Every chapter's `spec.md` states its governed scope, its failure modes, and what it deliberately does not cover.
`transfer.md` states the transfer target and how the two forms differ.
The `case-data.md` file is the frozen case; **the manuscript may not contradict it**, and if you find a place where it does, that is a defect worth reporting.

Two cautions.

**The exercises have no pilot data.** Nothing in the assessment design has been tested on a reader.

**The case has not been reviewed by a water professional.** It is fictional and internally consistent, and every figure in it is synthetic. It should not be presented to students as representative of utility practice.
