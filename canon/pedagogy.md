## Chapter 1 scaffold sequence

- Status: pedagogical synthesis used for authoring control; not a reader-facing named framework
- Purpose: move a novice from an initial unsupported framing attempt to independent production of a first-pass analysis in an unfamiliar domain
- Built from established concepts: complete worked examples; prompted self-explanation; progressive fading of reasoning support; structurally designed contrasts; error diagnosis and repair; retrieval practice; independent transfer assessment; delayed retrieval/transfer practice
- First used in: Chapter 1 exercise and worked-example architecture
- Caution: no single source validates the exact combined sequence `attempt → worked example → self-explanation → fading → error diagnosis → independent cold transfer → retrieval → delayed retest`; the integration is this book's synthesis. Exact values such as a five-minute opening attempt, three self-explanation pauses, a 50% active-learning guardrail, a 30–40 minute transfer task, and a 7–14 day retest window are design parameters pending pilot validation rather than universal learning-science optima. Scaffolds must eventually disappear; performance that depends on a visible checklist, worked solution, or rubric does not by itself demonstrate independent transfer.
